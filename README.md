Eyvind's Extended Jukebox Vol 01
---------------------------------

79 high-quality sids using one disk side (D64).
Here is Vol 01. Now improved with a sid-scanner.
The sid-scanner will produce visual data from the sid, to display on screen when playing.

How to use:
Use joystick in port 2. Up and down navigates the list of sids.
Joy left; changes the playmode.
Joy right; toggles the scanner on/off.

Playmode:
  CONTINUE will play the current sid to its end,
  then play the next sid in the list.

  RANDOM will play the current sid to its end,
  then play a random sid after that.

  LOOP will play the sid over and over.
  If the scanner is on in this mode, a maximum of roughly 7 mins
  will be looped.

While playing you can press the firebutton to get back to the menu.
Joy down will play the next sid in the list, or the next random sid
depending on the playmode.
